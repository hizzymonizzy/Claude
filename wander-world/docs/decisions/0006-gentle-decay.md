# 0006 Decay is gentle and tied to the weekly goal

**Status.** Superseded by [0008](0008-weekly-cooling.md) (2026-11-21).

**Decision.** The weekly goal uses Health Connect intensity minutes. At 80% of the goal, decay slows; at 100%,
decay stops; up to 150%, things are restored. The goal adapts: it rises slowly and falls quickly, within 150–300.
Settlements fade to ruins but are never erased. The capital never drops below Village once it reaches it. Rest
mode and distant (dormant) regions protect the world.

**Why.** A health app must not punish rest, injury or travel. History is preserved.
