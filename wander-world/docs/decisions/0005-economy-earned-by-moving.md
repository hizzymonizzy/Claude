# 0005 Resources are earned by moving

**Decision.** Gold comes from weekly-goal intensity plus distance, and it is never reduced for routine. Wood,
food, stone and metal come from places and streets. Each source thins with each day it's harvested and grows
back with rest, and the first harvest of a new source pays double (alpha 0.9.0). No trading, waiting or buying.

**Why.** Reward exploration over frequenting, without punishing the habit the app exists to build.
